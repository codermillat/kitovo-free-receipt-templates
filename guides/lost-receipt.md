# Lost a receipt? What to do

For expense reimbursement, most employers and tax rules accept a written
declaration when the original receipt is genuinely lost — as long as you
document the transaction honestly and promptly.

A missing-receipt declaration should state:

- Your name and the date of the original purchase
- The seller, what was bought, and the amount paid
- How you paid
- That the original receipt was lost, and that you are not claiming the
  expense anywhere else
- Your signature and the date of the declaration
