# EU & UK receipts and VAT reverse charge

For B2B services supplied cross-border inside the EU, the supplier usually
does **not** charge VAT — the customer accounts for it under the reverse
charge mechanism. The receipt/invoice should:

- Show both parties' names, addresses and VAT IDs
- State the net amount and 0% VAT
- Carry a clear reverse-charge note, e.g. "Reverse charge — VAT to be
  accounted for by the recipient"
- Use the customer's currency and details consistently

For exports outside the EU/UK, 0% / zero-rated treatment follows the same
documentation habit: keep the parties, amounts and the reason for the 0%
rate explicit on the document.
