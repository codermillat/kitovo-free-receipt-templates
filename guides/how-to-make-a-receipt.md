# How to make a receipt

A receipt needs, at minimum:

1. The seller's / issuer's name and contact details
2. A receipt number and the date of the transaction
3. The buyer's name (for rent, services and B2B receipts)
4. Each item or service, its quantity and price
5. Subtotal, any tax, and the total paid
6. The payment method (cash, transfer, card) and, for transfers, the reference
7. A signature or stamp where the other party needs one
