# Rent receipts and HRA (India)

Tenants claiming House Rent Allowance (HRA) under Section 10(13A) need rent
receipts from their landlord. A valid rent receipt shows:

- Tenant and landlord names
- The rented address
- The rental period and the monthly rent amount
- The landlord's signature; landlord PAN is required when annual rent
  exceeds the threshold in the current rules
- A revenue stamp where applicable

Note: HRA exemption is available under the old tax regime — check which
regime you are filing under before relying on rent receipts.
